Privacy notice

This notice explains how Insight Pros, a JEMST Consulting company, handles information sent through this website.

What we collect

When you send the inquiry form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, business name, the service you are interested in and your message. We also record the page you sent it from.

If you book a consultation, the booking is handled by Calendly, which has its own privacy policy.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your inquiry and to discuss the services you asked about. We do not sell your information.

How it is stored

Form submissions are delivered to our business email and stored in our internal records so we can follow up. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Questions about this notice or your information: email info@jemst-consulting.com or call 863-259-3434.
