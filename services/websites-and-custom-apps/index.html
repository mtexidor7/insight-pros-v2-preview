Service 02 · What is included

Websites and custom apps, built around your business.

  • Business websites

    Clear service pages, contact paths and local details, written so customers and search engines understand what you do.

  • Landing pages

    Focused pages for one offer or campaign, built to turn ad clicks into calls, bookings or quote requests.

  • Online stores

    Product pages, checkout and order notifications on a platform your team can manage.

  • Dashboards

    One screen that pulls numbers from your tools, so you can see leads, jobs or sales without exporting spreadsheets.

  • Internal tools

    Custom apps for tasks your current software does not handle well, such as intake, scheduling or tracking.

  • Fast on every device

    Compressed images, clean code and layouts tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

How it works

Four clear steps.

  1. DiscoveryWe learn what the site or tool needs to accomplish and who will use it.
  2. Structure and designWe map the pages or screens, write or refine the copy and design the look.
  3. Build and connectWe build it, connect forms and tools, and test on real devices.
  4. Launch and hand-offWe launch, show your team how to make updates and agree on support.
A good fit if

Sound familiar?

  • You need a new website, or your current one is hard to update.
  • You run ads and need a page built for a single offer.
  • Your team copies information between tools by hand.
  • You want one dashboard instead of five browser tabs.

Questions

About websites and custom apps.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages and features. After the consultation we give you a timeline along with the written quote.

Can we update the website ourselves?

Yes. We set it up so your team can edit text and images, and we show you how.

Do you build custom apps or only websites?

Both. We also build dashboards and internal tools when existing software does not fit how you work.

Will the website work on phones?

Yes. Every page is designed and tested for phones, tablets and desktops.
