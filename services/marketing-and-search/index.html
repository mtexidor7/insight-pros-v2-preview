Questions

About marketing, seo and search visibility.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO helps your website and Google Business Profile appear in search results and maps. AEO structures your content so search features and voice assistants can quote a clear answer. GEO improves the chance that tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity describe your business accurately, starting with consistent business details across the web.

How long does SEO take?

SEO usually takes months, not weeks, and results vary by market and competition. Paid ads can bring traffic sooner while SEO builds.

Do you manage Google Ads and social ads?

Yes. We set up and manage Google Ads and paid social campaigns, and we agree on the budget with you before anything runs.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Search engines decide rankings, so no one can honestly guarantee them. We focus on accurate details, useful pages, technical fixes and reviews, and we report what changes.
