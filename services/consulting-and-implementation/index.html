Questions

About consulting and implementation.

What does a business review include?

A look at your website, search presence, lead handling, communication tools and repetitive tasks, followed by recommendations in priority order.

Do we have to hire you for implementation?

No. The plan is yours to keep. Many clients ask us to implement it, but that is your choice.

What does agreed support mean?

We write down the scope of ongoing help, such as updates, monitoring or a set number of hours, so both sides know what is included.

Do you work with larger organizations?

Yes. We work with small businesses, growing teams and larger organizations, and scale the review and implementation to fit.
