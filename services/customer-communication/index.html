Service 04 · What is included

Customer communication, built around your business.

  • Automated call answering

    A natural-sounding automated voice that answers common questions, takes messages, books appointments and transfers callers to your team. We set up a clear greeting so callers know it is automated.

  • Website chat

    A chat window on your website that answers common questions from your business details, collects contact information and books appointments.

  • Text and email replies

    Two-way text and email conversations in one inbox, with saved replies for common questions.

  • Missed-call text-back

    When you cannot pick up, the caller gets a text so the conversation can continue.

  • Booking

    Customers pick a time from your real availability, with confirmations sent automatically.

  • Follow-up

    Reminders, quote follow-ups and post-visit messages sent on a schedule you approve.

Automated tools work from the information you give them and can make mistakes. We set clear limits on what they handle, route anything unusual to your team, and review conversations with you after launch.

How it works

Four clear steps.

  1. Map your inquiriesWe list how customers reach you today and what they usually ask.
  2. Set up and connectWe configure each tool with your business details and connect it to your calendar and CRM.
  3. Test togetherYou call, text and chat with it before customers do.
  4. Launch and reviewWe go live and review conversations with you to improve answers.

Questions

About customer communication.

Is automated call answering a real person?

No. It is an automated voice set up with your business details. It can answer common questions, take messages and book appointments, and it can transfer callers to your team when a person is needed.

What happens when it cannot answer a question?

It takes a message or transfers the call to your team, based on rules we set together.

Does missed-call text-back work with our current phone number?

In many cases, yes, depending on your phone provider and setup. We check this during the consultation.

Can it book appointments into our calendar?

Yes, it can book into supported calendars and booking tools. We confirm compatibility with what you use before setup.
